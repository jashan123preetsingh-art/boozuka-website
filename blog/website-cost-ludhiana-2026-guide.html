In 30 seconds

  • There is no single "website price" in Ludhiana. Typical market ranges run from a few thousand rupees for a one-page site to well over a lakh for custom builds and online stores.
  • What you really pay for is scope: number of pages, custom design, content, integrations, SEO setup and who owns the code afterwards.
  • The cheapest quote often hides yearly renewals, plugin fees or a site you cannot move.
  • Ask for a written scope, ownership terms and the full first-year cost before you pay anything.

Who this is for: shop owners, clinics, institutes, manufacturers and startups in Ludhiana and Punjab planning a new website or a redesign in 2026.

Why quotes in Ludhiana vary so much

Ask five people in Ludhiana what a website costs and you will hear five numbers. A freelancer on Instagram may say ₹5,000. A local agency may say ₹40,000. A software house may say ₹2 lakh. All three can be honest, because they are quoting for different things.

Typical ranges seen in the Ludhiana market (October 2026) run from roughly ₹3,000 for a basic one-page site to ₹1 lakh and above for custom builds and online stores. These are broad, indicative market ranges, not a survey, and actual quotes vary with scope. The point is not the exact figure; it is that the same word "website" covers very different products.

Seven things that change the price

  1. Design: template or custom. A theme with your logo swapped in is quick. A design made around your customers, brand and offer takes research and more hours. Ask to see the design before development starts.
  2. Number of pages. Each service, location or product category page needs its own content and layout. Ten real pages cost more than three, but they also give Google more to rank.
  3. Content. Who writes the text and who arranges photos? Content is often the slowest part of a project. If the agency writes it, it should be in the quote.
  4. Integrations. WhatsApp click-to-chat, enquiry forms to email, Google Maps, booking calendars, payment gateways, CRMs or sheets. Every connection is work and something to test.
  5. SEO foundations. Proper titles, descriptions, headings, schema markup, a sitemap, fast loading and Search Console setup. These are cheap to do at build time and expensive to fix later.
  6. Speed and hosting. A heavy page builder on cheap shared hosting can load slowly on mobile data. Static or well-built sites on a good host load fast and cost less to run.
  7. Ownership and support. Do you get the code, the domain and the hosting login? Is there support after launch, and for how long?
Tip

Write down your must-haves before calling anyone: pages, features, deadline and budget range. You will get quotes you can actually compare.

Hidden costs to ask about

A low upfront price is sometimes recovered later. Ask about each of these in writing:

  • Domain and hosting renewals. "Free domain and hosting" is often free only for the first year, then renewed at a higher rate.
  • Paid plugins and themes. Premium WordPress plugins and themes usually need yearly licences.
  • Platform fees. Shopify and similar platforms charge a monthly plan, and some apps add more fees.
  • Change requests. How many rounds of changes are included, and what does an extra change cost?
  • Lock-in. If the site is built on the agency's own panel, you may not be able to move it.
  • Email. Business email on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 is a separate per-user subscription.
Common mistake

Letting the developer register the domain in their own name. Always make sure the domain is registered to you or your company, with your email.

When a cheap website is fine and when it is not

A basic site is perfectly fine if you only need people who already know your name to find your phone number and location. Plenty of shops get most customers from Google Maps and WhatsApp, and a simple page plus a strong Google Business Profile is enough.

A cheap site becomes expensive when you want the website itself to bring new enquiries: when you run Google or Meta ads to it, when you want to rank for searches like "IELTS coaching in Ludhiana" or "hosiery manufacturer Ludhiana", or when you sell to buyers outside Punjab who judge you only by your site. In those cases, speed, clear copy and trust signals directly affect how many people contact you.

What Ludhiana businesses usually need

Manufacturers and exporters

Hosiery, bicycle parts and machinery units need a product catalogue, spec sheets, an enquiry form per product and pages that look credible to buyers in other states and countries.

Institutes and clinics

IELTS centres, coaching institutes and clinics need fast mobile pages, course or treatment pages, demo or appointment booking and WhatsApp follow-up.

Shops and D2C brands

Apparel and lifestyle brands need product photos that load fast, UPI and card payments, shipping rules and GST-ready invoices.

Notice that each one costs differently because each needs different pages and features, not because one agency is fair and another is greedy.

How to compare two quotes fairly

  1. Put both quotes in the same table: pages, design type, content, integrations, SEO tasks, timeline, support period.
  2. Ask for live examples of similar work and open them on your phone on mobile data.
  3. Check ownership: code, domain, hosting and analytics in your name.
  4. Ask for the first-year total, including renewals and subscriptions, not just the build fee.
  5. Ask who you will talk to during the project and how changes are handled.

Setting a sensible budget

Instead of starting with a number, start with what the website must do in the next 12 months. If it must bring 20 new enquiries a month, work backwards: which searches or ads will bring visitors, which pages they will land on, and what will make them message you. That tells you how many pages, what content and which integrations you need, and the budget follows from that.

If your budget is tight, launch smaller but better: five fast, well-written pages with correct SEO beat twenty thin pages. You can add service and location pages later.

Common questions

How much does a website cost in Ludhiana in 2026?

It depends on scope. Typical ranges seen in the Ludhiana market (Oct 2026) start from a few thousand rupees for a one-page site and go past a lakh; actual quotes vary for custom builds and online stores. Compare quotes on pages, design, content, integrations, SEO and ownership, not just the headline number.

Is WordPress cheaper than a custom-coded site?

Often at the start, because themes are ready-made. Yearly plugin licences, updates and slower speed can make it cost more over time. A simple static site can be cheaper to run if you do not need to edit it daily.

How long does a business website take?

A focused 5–10 page site usually takes two to four weeks once content is ready. Content and feedback delays are the most common reason projects run late.

Do I need to pay every year?

Usually yes for the domain and hosting, and sometimes for plugins, platform plans and email. Ask for the yearly running cost in writing.

Should SEO be included in the website price?

Basic on-page SEO, such as titles, descriptions, headings, schema, sitemap, speed and Search Console, should be part of any professional build. Ongoing SEO work, such as content and local listings, is a separate monthly service.
