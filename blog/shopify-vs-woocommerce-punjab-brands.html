Shopify vs WooCommerce for Punjab brands: which should you choose?

By Jashanpreet Singh, founder of Boozuka · 25 October 2026 · 4 min read

In 30 seconds

  • Shopify is usually the faster, lower-maintenance choice for apparel, hosiery and D2C brands in Punjab that want to start selling online quickly.
  • WooCommerce gives more control and can cost less per month, but needs more upkeep: hosting, updates, security and plugins.
  • A custom store makes sense only for unusual B2B needs like dealer pricing, bulk ordering or ERP integration.
  • Whatever you choose, product photos, clear shipping and return rules, and WhatsApp support decide sales more than the platform.

Who this is for: clothing, hosiery, footwear, sports goods, food and lifestyle brands in Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Amritsar and across Punjab planning an online store.

Why this question comes up so often in Punjab

Ludhiana is one of India's biggest hosiery and knitwear centres, Jalandhar is known for sports goods, and Amritsar has a strong market for traditional wear and food products. Many of these businesses already sell through dealers, marketplaces or Instagram, and want their own store so they keep margins and customer relationships. The first question is almost always: Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify in plain language

Shopify is a hosted platform. You pay a monthly plan and Shopify runs the servers, security and checkout. You choose a theme, add products and connect payments and shipping.

  • Quick to launch and easy for your staff to add products and manage orders from a phone
  • Hosting, security and updates are handled by Shopify
  • Works with Indian payment gateways, UPI and popular shipping aggregators through apps
  • Large app store for reviews, WhatsApp, COD verification and more
  • Monthly plan fee, plus paid apps that add up
  • Less control over deep customisation without developer help
  • You build on Shopify's platform, so moving away later takes work

WooCommerce in plain language

WooCommerce is a free plugin that turns a WordPress website into a store. You or your agency handle hosting, updates and security.

  • No platform subscription for the core software
  • Full control over the code, data and design
  • Good if you also want a content-heavy site or blog on WordPress
  • Needs good hosting, regular updates and backups
  • Premium plugins and themes often need yearly licences
  • Can become slow if loaded with plugins on cheap hosting

India-specific things to set up on either platform

  1. Payments. UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through an Indian gateway. Test refunds too.
  2. Cash on delivery rules. COD is still common. Consider COD limits, OTP or WhatsApp confirmation to reduce fake orders and returns.
  3. GST. Correct tax rates on products, GSTIN on invoices and invoice numbering. Confirm details with your CA.
  4. Shipping. Pin-code serviceability, delivery estimates and a shipping partner that suits your order volume.
  5. Policies. Clear shipping, return, exchange and refund pages. These build trust and are required by payment gateways.
  6. WhatsApp. Order confirmations, shipping updates and a chat button for size or fabric questions.
Common mistake

Launching with phone photos on mixed backgrounds and no size chart. For apparel, a consistent photo style and an accurate size chart reduce returns more than any app.

What about B2B and wholesale?

Many Ludhiana manufacturers sell mainly to dealers and retailers. They need tiered pricing, minimum order quantities, bulk enquiry forms and sometimes catalogue PDFs per product. Both platforms can handle simple B2B with apps or plugins. If you need dealer logins with their own price lists, credit limits or ERP sync, a custom web app or portal may be more suitable, sometimes alongside a Shopify store for retail customers.

How to decide in 10 minutes

  • Choose Shopify if you want to launch quickly, your team is not technical and you prefer predictable monthly fees.
  • Choose WooCommerce if you already run WordPress, need deep customisation and have someone reliable to maintain it.
  • Choose custom only if your ordering process genuinely does not fit either platform.

Launch checklist

  1. Domain connected and SSL active
  2. At least your best 20–30 products with good photos, prices, sizes and descriptions
  3. Payments and COD tested with real orders and a refund
  4. Shipping zones, charges and delivery estimates set
  5. Policy pages published and linked in the footer
  6. Google Search Console, Merchant Center and analytics connected
  7. Instagram and WhatsApp linked to the store

Then drive traffic: product-focused Meta and Google ads, SEO for category pages and a strong brand identity so your store does not look like every other reseller.

Common questions

Is Shopify good for clothing brands in Ludhiana?

Yes, for most D2C apparel and hosiery brands it is the quickest way to launch a reliable store with Indian payments, COD and shipping apps, with low maintenance.

Is WooCommerce cheaper than Shopify?

The core software is free, but hosting, premium plugins, themes and maintenance cost money. Over a year the total can be similar; it depends on your setup.

Can I sell wholesale on Shopify?

Simple wholesale, such as bulk pricing and minimum order quantities, can be done with apps. Complex dealer portals usually need custom development.

Do I still need Amazon or Flipkart?

Marketplaces bring ready buyers, your own store builds your brand and margins. Many brands use both, with their own store as the home base.
