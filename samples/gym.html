Gym management software, built for the front desk

Ironhaus OS is a working demo we built for a fictional gym in Ludhiana. Members, renewals, check-ins, payments, GST invoices and leads in one fast screen.

Open live demo →Get one for your business

No sign-up needed. All data is made up and stays in your browser. Reset any time.

What the demo does

Eight screens you can click through, built the way we build real client software.

  • DashboardActive members, renewals due, today's check-ins and this month's collection, with a revenue trend, attendance heatmap, plan mix and an alerts feed.
  • MembersSearch and filter 148 demo members. Open any profile for plan, expiry, payments, attendance, a progress chart and notes. Add or edit members.
  • RenewalsSee who is due in 7, 15 or 30 days and open a ready-made WhatsApp reminder in one tap.
  • Check-in kioskA front-desk screen where members type their PIN to check in.
  • Payments and GST invoicesEvery payment by mode, with an invoice preview that splits CGST and SGST.
  • Leads boardDrag enquiries from New to Trial to Joined or Lost.
  • Trainers and classesThe weekly class schedule with seats left, and the trainers who run them.
  • Workout, reports and settingsA workout and diet builder, simple reports, a light or dark theme, and a Ctrl+K command palette.

Every member on one screen

Open a profile to see plan, expiry, payments and attendance. Drag leads across the board as they move.

Works on the phone too

Owners check numbers on the go. Below 900px the app switches to a bottom tab bar, and every screen fits a 360px phone.
