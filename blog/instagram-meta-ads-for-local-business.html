Meta ads

Instagram and Meta ads for local businesses in Punjab

By Jashanpreet Singh, founder of Boozuka · 21 October 2026 · 2 min read

On this page

  1. Pick the right goal
  2. What a good local ad looks like
  3. Targeting
  4. The follow-up matters most

In 30 seconds

  • Target people near your business.
  • Use one clear offer per ad.
  • Send people to WhatsApp or a short form.
  • Reply fast, or the ad money is wasted.

Who this is for: shops, clinics, institutes, gyms, salons, cafes, showrooms and service businesses in Punjab and across India.

Pick the right goal

For local businesses, choose "messages" or "leads", not "boost post". You want conversations, not just views.

What a good local ad looks like

  • Real photo or short video of your place or work
  • One clear offer, like a free trial or demo class
  • Your area name in the text
  • A clear button: Send WhatsApp message

Targeting

Choose a radius around your business. Keep it simple at first. Let Meta find people, then check which ads bring real enquiries.

The follow-up matters most

Most ad leads are lost because nobody replies quickly. Set up an instant WhatsApp reply and a lead sheet before you spend.

Checklist

  • Goal set to messages or leads
  • Offer clear
  • Radius set
  • Auto-reply ready
  • Lead sheet ready

Common questions

How much should I spend?

Start small, test for a week or two, then increase what works.

Instagram or Facebook?

Run both from one Meta ad account and let results decide.

Should I boost posts?

Boosting gets views. For enquiries, create proper message or lead ads.
