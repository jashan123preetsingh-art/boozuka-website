Ironhaus OS: gym management software, built for the front desk

A full gym CRM we designed and built as a working demo for a fictional gym in Ludhiana. Members, renewals, check-ins, GST invoices, leads, reports and a member QR pass in one fast web app.

Screen tour

Ten screens, one system

Tap through the screens below, or open the live demo and click around yourself.

The whole gym at a glance

Active members, renewals due, today's check-ins and this month's collection, with a revenue trend, attendance heatmap, plan mix and live alerts.

Every member, one click away

Search and filter members, then open a profile for plan, expiry, payments, attendance, a progress chart and notes. Renew in one tap.

Never miss a renewal

See who is due in 7, 15 or 30 days and send a ready-made WhatsApp reminder. The template is editable in settings.

PIN kiosk at the front desk

Members type their 4-digit PIN on a tablet kiosk. Every visit feeds attendance, streaks and the peak-hours report.

GST invoices, done right

Every payment by mode, with a tax invoice that splits CGST and SGST and prints to PDF.

A sales board for enquiries

Drag leads from New to Trial to Joined or Lost, with the source of every enquiry tracked.

Numbers you can act on

Retention, visits per member, revenue by plan, peak hours and lead sources. Export members, payments and leads to CSV.

A QR pass in every pocket

Members see a QR pass, days left on their plan, their visit streak and the next class to book.

Make it yours

Gym profile, plan prices, GST details and WhatsApp templates with a live message preview.

Nothing slips through

A notifications panel for renewals, expired plans, new leads and classes that are nearly full.
