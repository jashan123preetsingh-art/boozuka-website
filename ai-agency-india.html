Where AI actually helps a small business

Answering common questions

Timings, fees, location, documents needed. AI answers in seconds, any time of day.

Sorting enquiries

AI reads each message and tags it: hot lead, price question, complaint or spam.

Drafting replies and posts

AI writes a first draft. Your team edits and sends it.

See a sample of how this flows on our home page workflow demo.

What AI should not do

  • Make promises to customers on your behalf
  • Handle payments or refunds without a person
  • Give medical, legal or financial advice
  • Send messages to people who never contacted you
Our rule

A person reviews anything that matters before it goes out.

How we set it up

  1. Find the task. We list what your team repeats every day.
  2. Build a small version. One workflow, tested on real but safe examples.
  3. Train your team. A short session in Hindi, Punjabi or English.
  4. Improve monthly. We check what the AI got wrong and fix it.
