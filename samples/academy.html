03

How it works

A simple weekly rhythm

You always know what is next, and so do your parents.

  1. LearnFour skill classes a week: listening, reading, writing and speaking.
  2. PractiseTimed tasks in class and short daily practice at home.
  3. TestA full-length mock test every Saturday, in exam conditions.
  4. ImproveWritten feedback on essays and a one-to-one speaking slot.
04

Your first six weeks

A clear plan, from day one to exam day

This is how a typical IELTS batch runs. Your trainer adjusts it after your baseline test.

  1. Week 1

    Baseline test and your plan

    A short test in all four skills. You get a written plan for the next five weeks.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Skill classes and daily practice

    Four skill classes a week, plus 30 minutes of practice at home each day.

  3. Week 4

    Full mock in exam conditions

    Timed, invigilated and marked, with feedback on every module.

  4. Week 5

    Fix the weak spots

    Extra writing feedback and a one-to-one speaking slot.

  5. Week 6

    Final mock and exam-day plan

    A last full test, a checklist for the day, and a review with your trainer.
