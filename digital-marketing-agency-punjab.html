How to choose the right agency in Punjab

  • Ask to see the actual work, not just a nice presentation.
  • Ask who will do the work: an in-house team or freelancers.
  • Avoid anyone who promises the number one spot on Google.
  • Check that you will own your website, ad account and Google profile.
