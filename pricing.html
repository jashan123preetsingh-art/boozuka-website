Ask us on WhatsApp
Why do you show "starts from" prices?+

Two salons or two institutes rarely need the same work. The starting price is for the most common scope. After the free audit you get a fixed quote for your scope, so there are no surprises later.

Can I take just one service?+

Yes. You can pick just one service, such as a website, the Google SEO Pack or a WhatsApp CRM, or mix several. Care plans are optional. The final quote comes after a free audit.

How does the 3-Month Google SEO Pack work?+

It's a complete Google SEO and Google Maps package: ₹24,000 for 3 months, offered till 30 Nov 2026. We optimise your Google Business Profile, improve on-page SEO and local keywords, build citations, set up a reviews system, add posts and photos to your profile, and send monthly progress reports. Rankings depend on competition and are not guaranteed.

What is the 3-month Full Growth plan?+

A marketing-first plan for businesses ready to scale. It covers digital marketing, Google SEO, Google and Meta ads management and social media management for three months, starting from ₹30,000 a month. Combo plans look after your website, Google profile and WhatsApp/CRM with marketing on top; Full Growth puts the whole team on marketing.

How do ad budgets work?+

Your ad budget is set with you and is paid directly from your own Google/Meta account, so you stay in control of every rupee.

How much do the combo plans cost?+

Starter starts from ₹19,999 a month, Growth from ₹29,999 and Premium from ₹39,999. Your final fee comes after the free audit.

How is custom work priced?+

A business CRM or custom software starts from ₹30,000 and usually falls between ₹30,000 and ₹50,000, depending on scope, with an optional care plan of ₹3,000 a month. Personal software and one-off projects get a custom quote after a free audit.

Is GST included?+

Prices shown exclude GST. If GST applies, it is shown separately in your proposal.

How does payment work?+

For projects, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are billed in advance.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. Results depend on many things outside anyone's control. We commit to clear work, honest reporting and quick fixes when something isn't working.

What is the free audit?+

A 20-minute call where we look at your Google listing, website, WhatsApp replies, Instagram and reviews, and tell you the top fixes. There is no obligation to buy.
