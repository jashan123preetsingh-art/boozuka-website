AI automation agency: let the repeated work run on its own

By the Boozuka team, Ludhiana · Updated 21 October 2026

Boozuka is a tech, design and AI company in Ludhiana that also runs marketing: we build websites and software, design your brand, automate follow-ups with AI, and grow your enquiries with SEO and ads.

Every business has tasks that happen the same way every day. We connect your tools so those tasks happen automatically, and your team only steps in when a person is needed.

In 30 seconds

  • Automate enquiries, follow-ups, reminders and weekly reports.
  • We use n8n, Make, Google Apps Script and WhatsApp Business.
  • You own every account and every automation we build.
  • Start with one workflow, then add more.

Who this is for: shops, clinics, institutes, gyms, salons, cafes, showrooms and service businesses in Punjab and across India.

Automations we set up most often

  • A new website or ad enquiry goes into a Google Sheet and the right person gets a WhatsApp alert
  • Auto-reply on WhatsApp within seconds, with options like fees, timings or booking
  • Follow-up reminders on day 1, day 3 and day 7 so no lead goes cold
  • Renewal and fee reminders for gyms, institutes and clinics
  • A weekly summary email: new leads, follow-ups done and what is pending

Common questions

Do I need new software?

Usually not. We build on Google Sheets, Gmail and WhatsApp Business that you already use. When a business needs more, we build custom software like our gym CRM sample.

What if an automation breaks?

We set up simple alerts and fix issues as part of support.

Can you automate WhatsApp bulk messages?

Only within WhatsApp rules: messages to people who opted in, using approved templates. We do not do spam.

How long does setup take?

A first workflow usually takes one to two weeks, depending on how many tools are involved.
