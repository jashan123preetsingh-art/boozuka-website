Questions, answered

Ask us on WhatsApp →
Why do you show "starts from" prices?+

Two salons or two institutes rarely need the same work. The starting price is for the most common scope. After the free audit you get a fixed quote for your scope, so there are no surprises later.

Can I take just one service?+

Yes. You can pick just one service, such as a website, the Google SEO Pack or a WhatsApp CRM, or mix several. Care plans are optional. The final quote comes after a free audit.

How does the 3-Month Google SEO Pack work?+

It's a complete Google SEO and Google Maps package: ₹24,000 for 3 months, offered till 30 Nov 2026. We optimise your Google Business Profile, improve on-page SEO and local keywords, build citations, set up a reviews system, add posts and photos to your profile, and send monthly progress reports. Rankings depend on competition and are not guaranteed.

What is the 3-month Full Growth plan?+

A marketing-first plan for businesses ready to scale. It covers digital marketing, Google SEO, Google and Meta ads management and social media management for three months, starting from ₹31,999 a month. Combo plans look after your website, Google profile and WhatsApp/CRM with marketing on top; Full Growth puts the whole team on marketing.

How do ad budgets work?+

Your ad budget is set with you and is paid directly from your own Google/Meta account, so you stay in control of every rupee.

How much do the combo plans cost?+

Plans start from ₹9,999 a month with Essential. Starter starts from ₹19,999, Growth from ₹29,999 and Premium from ₹42,999. Your final fee comes after the free audit.

How is custom work priced?+

A business CRM or custom software starts from ₹34,999 and usually falls between ₹34,999 and ₹50,000, depending on scope, with an optional care plan of ₹3,499 a month. Web apps, SaaS and MVPs start from ₹42,999, mobile apps from ₹64,999, a WhatsApp CRM from ₹21,999 and a multi-product Shopify store with payment gateway from ₹31,999. Every project gets a fixed written quote after a free audit.

Is GST included?+

Prices shown exclude GST. If GST applies, it is shown separately in your proposal.

Is there a bundle discount?+

Yes. Take any two monthly services (Social media, Ads, Video editing or SEO) together and get 10% off the monthly fee for the first 3 months. Book a 3-month Full Growth or Premium plan and the landing page is free. Both offers need a 3-month commitment, and the terms are confirmed in your written scope.

What does social media management include?+

It is all-inclusive, starting from ₹16,999 a month: content planning, graphics, reel and post editing, captions and posting, plus a monthly report. There are no extra editing charges. Only paid promotion (ad spend) is extra. Separate video editing packs are for clients who only need editing.

How do video editing packs work?+

Video editing is sold in monthly packs only, with a minimum of 15 reels: 15 reels a month from ₹14,999 or 30 reels a month from ₹26,999. We don't edit single reels.

What are the payment terms?+

For projects, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are billed in advance. The SEO Pack takes the first 2 months (₹16,000) upfront and the balance at the start of month 3. Prices exclude GST.

How many revisions are included?+

The number of revision rounds is written into your scope before work starts, so you know exactly what is covered. Extra rounds or new scope are quoted first, never added silently.

What is not included?+

Ad spend (paid by you directly to Meta or Google), third-party costs such as domain, hosting, paid plugins, WhatsApp API message charges, stock assets and printing, and anything outside the written scope. We list these in your proposal.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. Results depend on many things outside anyone's control. We commit to clear work, honest reporting and quick fixes when something isn't working.

What is the free audit?+

A 30-minute call where we look at your Google listing, website, WhatsApp replies, Instagram and reviews, and tell you the top fixes. There is no obligation to buy.
