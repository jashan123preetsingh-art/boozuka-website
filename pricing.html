Ask us on WhatsApp
Why do you show ranges and "starting from" prices?+

Two salons or two institutes rarely need the same work. The starting price is for the most common scope. After the audit you get a fixed quote for your scope, so there are no surprises later.

Is GST included?+

Prices shown exclude GST. If GST applies, it is shown separately in your proposal.

How does payment work?+

For projects, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are billed in advance.

Is ad spend included in monthly plans?+

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google from your account. Our fee covers planning, creatives, management and reporting.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. Sales depend on many things we don't control. We commit to clear work, clear reporting and quick fixes when something isn't working.

What is the free audit?+

A 20-minute call where we look at your Google listing, website, WhatsApp replies, Instagram and reviews, and tell you the top fixes. There is no obligation to buy.
