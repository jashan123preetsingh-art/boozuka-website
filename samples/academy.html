How it works

Four steps from first class to exam day.

01/ 04
  1. 01

    Baseline test

    Day one starts with a short test in all four skills, so your plan starts from where you actually are.

  2. 02

    Learn in small batches

    Four skill classes a week in a small group, with timed practice in class and 30 minutes at home each day.

  3. 03

    Mock every Saturday

    A full-length mock test in exam conditions every week. Every score goes into your personal tracker.

  4. 04

    One-to-one feedback

    Line-by-line essay marking and a private speaking slot, so you fix the exact things holding your score back.

06

FAQ

Questions students ask

Still unsure? Message the institute on WhatsApp and a counsellor replies within the day.

Do you have online classes?

Yes. Every course runs offline in the institute and online on live video, with the same mock tests.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. You sit in on a full class with a real batch. Fees are shared on WhatsApp after the class, with no pressure to join.

Can I switch batches later?

Yes, if there is a seat in the other batch. Most students switch within the first two weeks.

How big are the batches?

Small, so the trainer can check every student's work in class. Exact numbers are shared on your demo day.

Do you help with test booking?

Yes. We help you pick a test date and book it once your mock scores are where you want them.
