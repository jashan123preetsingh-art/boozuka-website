Ask us on WhatsApp
Why do you show "starts from" prices?+

Two salons or two institutes rarely need the same work. The starting price is for the most common scope. After the free audit you get a fixed quote for your scope, so there are no surprises later.

How much do the combo plans cost?+

Starter starts from ₹19,999 a month, Growth from ₹29,999 and Premium from ₹39,999. Ad spend is separate. Your final fee comes after the free audit.

Can I take just one service?+

Yes. You can pick just one service, such as a website or a WhatsApp CRM, or mix several. Maintenance is optional and only if you need it. The final quote comes after a free audit.

What is the 3-month Full Growth plan?+

A marketing-first plan for businesses that want to scale. It covers digital marketing, Google SEO, Google and Meta ads management and social media management for three months, starting from ₹30,000 a month. Ad spend is paid separately. The combo plans focus on keeping your website, Google profile and WhatsApp/CRM running with basic marketing; Full Growth puts the whole team on marketing. Results depend on your market, and we don't guarantee them.

How does the Local SEO plan work?+

It's a 3-month plan that starts from ₹9,999 a month. We optimise your Google Business Profile, improve on-page SEO, build local citations, set up a reviews system and send monthly reports, to move your business up Google and Maps results in your city or area in Punjab. We don't guarantee a #1 ranking; results depend on competition. You can add a CRM to capture and follow up the enquiries.

How is custom work priced?+

A business CRM or custom software starts from ₹30,000 and usually falls between ₹30,000 and ₹50,000, depending on scope, with optional maintenance of ₹3,000 a month. Personal software and one-off projects get a custom quote after a free audit.

Is GST included?+

Prices shown exclude GST. If GST applies, it is shown separately in your proposal.

How does payment work?+

For projects, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are billed in advance.

Is ad spend included in monthly plans?+

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google from your account. Our fee covers planning, creatives, management and reporting.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. Sales depend on many things we don't control. We commit to clear work, clear reporting and quick fixes when something isn't working.

What is the free audit?+

A 20-minute call where we look at your Google listing, website, WhatsApp replies, Instagram and reviews, and tell you the top fixes. There is no obligation to buy.
