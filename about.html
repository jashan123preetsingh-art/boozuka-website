About Boozuka

Boozuka at a glance

Boozuka is a tech, design and AI services company based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India. It is a digital growth partner for any business that wants to go online and grow, from shops, salons, cafes and clinics to service businesses and training institutes. It builds websites, web apps and custom software, designs UI/UX and brands, sets up AI agents, chatbots and automation, and runs SEO, social media, ads and video, all delivered by an in-house team. Boozuka was founded by Jashanpreet Singh, with co-founder Megha Walia.
