What a brand identity includes

Logo system

A main logo, a small mark for icons and favicons, and colour versions for light and dark backgrounds.

Colours & type

A small palette and font pairing with clear rules, so anyone on your team can stay on-brand.

Brand kit

Ready files: SVG and PNG logos, social icons, templates and a short guideline PDF.

Social creatives, packaging and print

Once the identity is ready, we design the pieces you use every week.

  • Instagram and Facebook post and story templates.
  • Ad creatives for Meta and Google campaigns.
  • Packaging, labels, menus and brochures.
  • Business cards, letterheads and email signatures.

How we work

  1. Brand call. Who you serve, what makes you different, and the feeling you want.
  2. Directions. A few clear logo and colour directions, explained in plain words.
  3. Refine. We polish one direction with your feedback.
  4. Brand kit. All files and a simple guide, ready for print and digital.

Our own brand, as an example

Our own identity, the “B.” mark, follows the same method: one geometric letter and a gold square full stop that means work finished properly. You can see the full kit on our brand page.

Common questions

How long does a logo and identity take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly feedback comes in. We share a clear timeline in the written quote.

Will I own the logo files?

Yes. You get the final source files and full rights to use them for your business.

Can you refresh our existing logo instead of starting over?

Yes. Many businesses only need a cleaner version of their logo plus consistent colours and templates.

Do you also design for social media?

Yes. We design post templates and ad creatives, and our team can also run your social media and ads.
