What should we automate first?+

Start with a repetitive task that has a clear input and output: sorting enquiries, preparing reports, updating records, or following up with leads. We’ll scope the task together before recommending a build.

Can you work with the tools we already use?+

We work with Google Workspace, Sheets, Excel, n8n, Make, and other tools in our company profile. Integration depends on your platform’s API and permissions; we confirm feasibility during scoping.

Do you offer websites and marketing too?+

Yes. We deliver mobile-first websites, landing pages, SEO, social media management, creatives, and Meta or Google Ads management. You can engage us for one service or agree a connected scope.

How do you price a project?+

Quotes reflect the scope, volume, turnaround, and complexity. Options include a defined project, per-task or per-record work, and monthly support. Any third-party software, API usage, ad spend, and ongoing support should be agreed in the quote.

Can we try a small task before committing?+

Yes. Share a sample task or brief. We’ll agree the trial scope and any cost, so you can evaluate our quality and communication before a larger engagement.

How is our data handled?+

Your data is used for your task, and NDAs are welcome. Before work begins, we agree what access is needed and which tools will process the data. Access and credentials should be shared through an agreed secure channel.
