AI automation for small businesses in India: a simple guide

By Jashanpreet Singh, founder of Boozuka · 21 October 2026 · 2 min read

On this page

  2. Five tasks to automate first
  3. What to avoid
  4. How to start this week

In 30 seconds

  • Automation means a task happens on its own when something else happens.
  • AI helps with reading and writing: sorting messages, drafting replies.
  • Start with one task that wastes the most time.
  • Keep a person in charge of anything important.

Who this is for: shops, clinics, institutes, gyms, salons, cafes, showrooms and service businesses in Punjab and across India.

What it means, in plain words

Automation: "When a new enquiry comes, save it in the sheet and alert the team." AI: "Read this message and tell me if it is a hot lead." Together they remove a lot of daily typing and checking.

Five tasks to automate first

  1. Save every enquiry to one sheet
  2. Send an instant WhatsApp reply
  3. Remind your team to follow up
  4. Send fee or renewal reminders
  5. Get a weekly summary of leads

What to avoid

  • Letting AI send offers or prices without checking
  • Messaging people who never contacted you
  • Buying a big tool before you know what you need

How to start this week

Write down every task your team repeats. Pick the one that takes the most time and is the most boring. That is your first automation.

Checklist

  • One task chosen
  • Tools listed
  • Person in charge named
  • Test planned for one week

Common questions

Do I need coding?

No. Tools like n8n, Make and Google Apps Script can be set up by an agency, and you just use the result.

Is it safe?

Yes, if data stays in your own accounts and only needed tools are connected.

Will it work in Hindi or Punjabi?

AI tools can read and write in Hindi and Hinglish well. Punjabi support is improving.
