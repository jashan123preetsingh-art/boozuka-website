We already run ads. Why do we need this?+

Ads bring enquiries. Most institutes lose them after that, in slow replies and missed follow-ups. We fix the part after the click.

Do we need new software?+

No. The CRM is a Google Sheet your team already understands, connected to WhatsApp and your lead forms.

Will you guarantee admissions?+

No. Admissions depend on your courses, fees, faculty and counselling. We commit to clear work, honest reporting and quick fixes.

How soon can we go live?+

A simple setup usually takes 2–4 weeks. Timing before admission season matters, so talk to us early.

Can you work in Hindi and Punjabi?+

Yes. Ad copy, WhatsApp replies and reels can be in Hindi, Punjabi or English, whatever your students use.

Who owns the leads and accounts?+

You do. Ad accounts, website, sheet and all lead data stay with your institute.
