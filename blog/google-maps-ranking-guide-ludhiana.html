In 30 seconds

  • When people search "salon near me", Google shows 3 businesses on a map first.

Who this is for: shops, salons, cafes, clinics, gyms and institutes in Ludhiana and across Punjab.

How Google picks who shows on the map

Search "cafe near me" in Sarabha Nagar. Google shows a map with three places. Most calls and visits go to these three.

Google says it mainly looks at three things:

◎Relevance

Does your profile match what the person searched for?

↔Distance

How close are you to the person searching?

★Prominence

How well known and trusted are you? Reviews and your website help.

You can't change distance. But you can improve relevance and prominence. That is what this guide is about.

Be careful

Nobody can honestly promise you the number one spot. A full, active and correct profile simply gives you a better chance.

Step 1: Set up your Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile (GBP) is your free business listing on Google Maps and Search.

  1. Claim your listing at business.google.com.
  2. Use your real shop name, exactly as on your signboard.
  3. Pick the best main category. For example "Hair salon", not just "Beauty".
  4. Add your phone, timings and website.
  5. List your services with one short line each.
Common mistake

Adding extra words to your name, like "Best Salon Ludhiana Cheap". Google can suspend your profile for this.

Keep your details the same everywhere

Your name, address and phone should be exactly the same on Google, your website, Instagram and Justdial. Even a small difference confuses Google.

Step 2: Add real photos and post updates

People trust profiles with good photos. Add:

  • The outside of your shop, so people find you easily
  • The inside
  • Your team at work
  • Your products or finished work

Add a few new photos every month. Use Google posts to share offers or news every week or two.

Step 3: Get honest reviews

Reviews help you rank. They also help people decide to call you.

Do

  • Ask happy customers right after a good visit
  • Send your review link on WhatsApp
  • Reply to every review, good or bad

Don't

  • Buy reviews
  • Swap reviews with friends
  • Write reviews yourself

For a bad review, say sorry, explain how you will fix it, and continue the talk on the phone.

Step 4: Link a simple website

A website helps Google trust your business. Make sure it:

  • Opens fast and looks good on a phone
  • Shows your name, area, phone and timings
  • Has a section for each main service
  • Has a map and a WhatsApp or call button

See what we include in our Google Maps and local SEO service. (SEO means helping people find you on Google.)

Step 5: List your business on other sites

Add your business, with the same details, on sites people in India use:

  • Justdial and Sulekha
  • IndiaMART (if you sell to other businesses)
  • Bing Places and Apple Business Connect
  • Facebook and Instagram

Step 6: Turn views into calls

Being seen is only half the job. Make it easy to contact you:

  • Show your WhatsApp number clearly
  • Reply to messages and questions fast
  • Check your profile numbers once a month: calls, direction requests and website clicks

If replies are slow, you lose the customer anyway. That is why we also set up WhatsApp auto-replies.

Quick tips for your type of business

These are simple examples, not real clients.

Salon in Model Town

List every service, like haircut, colour and bridal. Add photos of real work, with permission. Turn on messages so people can ask for a slot.

Cafe in Sarabha Nagar

Keep your menu link and photos fresh. Update timings on festival days. Mark Wi-Fi or outdoor seating if you have them.

Clinic or gym

Show correct timings and a clear phone number. Post when you start a new offer or trial. Never post patient photos.

A 15-minute weekly habit

Doing a little every week works better than doing a lot once.

  • Reply to new reviews and questions
  • Add one or two new photos
  • Share one short post
  • Check that your timings and phone are still right

Common questions

How long until I show higher on Google Maps?

It depends on your area and competition. Many businesses see more views within a few weeks of fixing their profile. Busy areas take longer.

Can I show up in areas far from my shop?

Mostly no. Google shows businesses close to the person searching. Never use a fake address.

Do I need a website to show on Maps?

No, but it helps. A website makes Google trust you more and gives customers more reasons to choose you.

Can I hide my address?

Yes, if customers don't visit you. For example, a home tutor or a mobile service. You show a service area instead.
