How our UI/UX process works

  1. Understand. A short call about your users, your goals and what is not working today.
  2. Map the flow. We sketch the key journeys: enquiry, booking, checkout or daily admin tasks.
  3. Wireframes. Low-detail layouts so we agree on structure before colours.
  4. Visual design in Figma. Clickable prototype for mobile and desktop, in your brand.
  5. Build hand-off. Our own developers build it, so the design ships as designed.

Why design and build with one team

Many projects break between the designer and the developer. At Boozuka the same in-house team designs and builds, so spacing, states, loading and error screens are thought through before code is written.

  • Mobile-first, because most of your customers come from a phone.
  • Accessible colour contrast and readable text sizes.
  • Fast pages: design choices that keep the site light.
  • Figma files handed over to you at the end.

Common questions

Both. We can hand over Figma files only, but most clients let our in-house developers build the design as a website or web app.

Which tools do you use?

Figma for design and prototypes. For build we use modern web technology suited to the project.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?

Yes. We start with a quick review of what works and what does not, then redesign the key screens first.
