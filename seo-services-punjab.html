Local SEO across Punjab cities

Search behaviour differs by city. People in Ludhiana search for manufacturers and institutes, Amritsar has strong tourism and food searches, and Chandigarh and Mohali searches lean towards IT, healthcare and real estate. We build pages and Google Business Profile work around how people in your city actually search, rather than copying one template everywhere.

  • Ludhiana · Jalandhar · Amritsar
  • Chandigarh & Mohali · Patiala · Bathinda

Technical SEO we check on every site

  • Unique titles and meta descriptions, one H1 per page
  • Canonical tags, clean URLs and no duplicate pages
  • Schema markup: organisation, services, breadcrumbs and real FAQs
  • Mobile speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Sitemap with accurate dates, submitted to Google and Bing, plus IndexNow
  • Internal links between services, locations and guides
