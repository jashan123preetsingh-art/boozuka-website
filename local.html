The enquiry journey

Where local enquiries get lost

Most local businesses already get enquiries. The loss happens in the small gaps between seeing you and visiting you.

  1. Search

    They search "near me"

    An incomplete Google profile, few photos or old timings send them to the next listing.

    Leak: not found or not chosen
  2. Message

    They send a WhatsApp

    The message lands at 9 pm on a personal phone. Nobody sees it until the next afternoon.

    Leak: slow or no reply
  3. Follow-up

    They say "I'll think about it"

    Without a reminder, the conversation stops. No one remembers who asked about what.

    Leak: no follow-up
  4. Visit

    They walk in

    Visits are not tracked, so you can't tell which ads or posts actually brought people.

    Leak: no tracking

What we fix: a complete Google profile, an instant WhatsApp auto-reply, follow-up reminders, and one sheet where every enquiry is tracked.

Who this is for, and who it's not for

A good fit

  • Coaching & IELTS institutes that get ad or walk-in enquiries and lose track before admission.
  • Salons & spas that want bookings and reminders on WhatsApp, and more Google reviews.
  • Cafes & restaurants that want to show up on Maps and keep their photos and menu current.
  • Clinics that want appointment requests answered and tracked, not lost in a phone.
  • Gyms that run trial offers and need every trial enquiry followed up.
  • Shops & showrooms that want calls and "directions" clicks from people nearby.

Probably not a fit

  • You want a ₹5,000 website or "just 10 posts". We build systems, not one-off pieces.
  • You want a guaranteed #1 ranking. Nobody honest can promise that.
  • No one on your side can reply to enquiries or take calls. Follow-up needs a person.
  • You are not ready to share access to your Google profile, ads or WhatsApp Business.
