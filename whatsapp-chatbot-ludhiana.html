Why WhatsApp first in Ludhiana

In Ludhiana, customers would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill a form or wait on a call. A student asks about IELTS batches at 11 pm, a dealer asks for a rate list after his shop shuts, a patient asks if the clinic is open on Sunday. If nobody answers for a few hours, many of them message someone else.

A chatbot does not replace your staff. It makes sure every message gets a quick, correct first reply, collects the details your team needs, and hands over the conversations that need a person.

What your chatbot will do

  • Reply in seconds, day and night, with your timings, location, services and next steps
  • Understand normal messages such as "weekend batch hai?" or "rate list bhejo", not only menu numbers
  • Collect details: name, phone, what they need, preferred time and how they found you
  • Book demo classes, appointments, visits or call-backs, and confirm them in writing
  • Remind people before appointments, only if they agreed to receive messages
  • Hand over to the right person with the full chat visible
  • Save every lead in a Google Sheet or CRM so nothing is lost in a phone

What it will not do

  • Invent prices, discounts or promises you have not approved
  • Give medical, legal, visa or financial advice
  • Send bulk messages to people who never contacted you
  • Pretend to be a person when a customer asks
Our rule

If the bot is not sure, it says so and passes the chat to your team. A wrong confident answer costs more than a short wait.

Common questions

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Not necessarily. An existing business number can usually be moved to the WhatsApp Business Platform. It should be a dedicated business number, not a personal one, and it stays in your name.

Can the chatbot reply in Punjabi and Hindi?

Yes. Hindi, Hinglish and English work very well. Punjabi works for most common questions, and we test with your real customer messages before launch.

What if the bot gives a wrong answer?

It answers only from information you approve and hands over when it is unsure. We review conversations after launch and fix any gaps.

Can my staff still reply from WhatsApp?

Yes. Your team can take over any chat at any time and see the full conversation in a shared inbox.

Can I send offers to all my customers?

Only to people who agreed to receive messages, using WhatsApp-approved templates. Sending to everyone breaks WhatsApp's rules and can get the number restricted.

How long does setup take?

A tuned WhatsApp Business app can be ready in a few days. Platform and AI chatbots usually take one to three weeks, depending on Meta's verification and how many flows you need.

Do you work outside Ludhiana?

Yes, across Punjab and India. Most work happens on calls, and we can meet in Ludhiana when it helps.
