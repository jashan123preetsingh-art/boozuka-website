What should we automate first?+

Start with the task that costs you the most enquiries: replying to WhatsApp messages, following up with leads, or sending booking reminders. We’ll scope the task together before recommending a build.

Can you work with the tools we already use?+

We work with Google Workspace, Sheets, Excel, n8n, Make, WhatsApp Business, Meta and Google Ads. Integration depends on your platform’s API and permissions; we confirm it during the audit.

Do you offer websites and marketing too?+

Yes. We deliver mobile-first websites, landing pages, SEO, social media management, creatives, and Meta or Google Ads management. You can engage us for one service or agree a connected scope.

How do you price a project?+

Quotes reflect the scope, number of pages or channels, and how fast you need it. You get a fixed quote after a free audit. See our pricing page for starting prices. Your ad budget is set with you and is paid directly from your own Google/Meta account, so you stay in control of every rupee.

Can we start small before a bigger project?+

Yes. Most clients start with the Enquiry Fix Sprint, a one-time 7–10 day project (see our pricing page), before a website, CRM or monthly plan.

How is our data handled?+

Your accounts and data stay in your name and are used only for your project. Before work begins, we agree what access is needed, and you can remove it at any time.
