In 30 seconds

  • Automate enquiries, follow-ups, reminders and weekly reports.

Automations we set up most often

  • A new website or ad enquiry goes into a Google Sheet and the right person gets a WhatsApp alert

Common questions

Do I need new software?

Usually not. We build on Google Sheets, Gmail and WhatsApp Business that you already use. When a business needs more, we build custom software like our gym CRM sample.

What if an automation breaks?

We set up simple alerts and fix issues as part of support.

Can you automate WhatsApp bulk messages?

Only within WhatsApp rules: messages to people who opted in, using approved templates. We do not do spam.

How long does setup take?

A first workflow usually takes one to two weeks, depending on how many tools are involved.
