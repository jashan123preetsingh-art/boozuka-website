What we hear from institutes

Where institutes lose admissions

01

Ad leads go cold.

People fill your lead form, and nobody calls them back for two days. By then they have joined another institute.

02

WhatsApp chaos.

Enquiries sit across personal phones and groups. No one knows who replied, or what was promised.

03

No follow-up.

A parent asks about fees once. Without a reminder, the conversation simply stops.

04

A weak website.

No clear courses, fees, results policy or enquiry button. Visitors leave without asking.

05

Admissions in notebooks.

Walk-ins, calls and demo classes live in a register. Counting this month's admissions takes an afternoon.

Get your institute found on Google

When a student searches "IELTS coaching in Ludhiana" or "NEET coaching near me", Google shows three institutes on the map first. We work to put yours there, on Search and on Maps.

Honest note. No one can guarantee the #1 spot. Rankings usually take a few months and depend on how competitive your city and course are. We show you the work and the movement every month.

01

Google Business Profile

Correct categories, courses as services, real photos, weekly posts and answered Q&A. The profile Google shows first.

02

Reviews system

After each admission, an automated WhatsApp message asks the student for an honest Google review. No fake or paid reviews.

03

Local keyword pages

One page per course and city, like "IELTS coaching in Ludhiana" or "NEET coaching in Jalandhar", written for students, not robots.

04

Technical SEO

Fast, mobile-first pages, clean structure and schema markup for EducationalOrganization, Course and FAQ.

05

Citations & directories

Consistent name, address and phone on Justdial, Sulekha and other trusted listings, so Google trusts your details.

06

Content in Hindi & English

Course guides, exam tips and results posts (only with student permission) that answer what parents actually search.

07

Monthly ranking report

Where you rank for your key searches, profile calls and direction requests, and what we do next. Plain language.

Do you outsource?+

No. Development, SEO and marketing, video editing and support are all done by our own in-house team, led by founder Jashanpreet Singh.

We already run ads. Why do we need this?+

Ads bring enquiries. Most institutes lose them after that, in slow replies and missed follow-ups. We fix the part after the click.

Do we need new software?+

No. The CRM is a Google Sheet your team already understands, connected to WhatsApp and your lead forms.

Will you guarantee admissions?+

No. Admissions depend on your courses, fees, faculty and counselling. We commit to clear work, honest reporting and quick fixes.

How soon can we go live?+

A simple setup usually takes 2–4 weeks. Timing before admission season matters, so talk to us early.

Can you work in Hindi and Punjabi?+

Yes. Ad copy, WhatsApp replies and reels can be in Hindi, Punjabi or English, whatever your students use.

Who owns the leads and accounts?+

You do. Ad accounts, website, sheet and all lead data stay with your institute.
