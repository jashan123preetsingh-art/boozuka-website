About Boozuka

What is Boozuka?

Boozuka is an AI automation and digital marketing agency based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India. It helps coaching and skill-training institutes and local businesses get more enquiries and reply to every one, using WhatsApp chatbots and AI automation, websites, Google SEO and Maps, ads and simple CRMs, all delivered by an in-house team. Boozuka was founded by Jashanpreet Singh, with co-founder Megha Walia.

What is Boozuka?+

Boozuka is an AI automation and digital marketing agency based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India. It helps coaching and skill-training institutes and local businesses get more enquiries and reply to every one, using WhatsApp chatbots and AI automation, websites, Google SEO and Maps, ads and simple CRMs, all delivered by an in-house team. Boozuka was founded by Jashanpreet Singh, with co-founder Megha Walia.

Who founded Boozuka?+

Boozuka was founded by Jashanpreet Singh, who leads strategy and client relationships. Megha Walia is co-founder; she also owns Chahat Industries and leads business strategy and client relationships at Boozuka.

Where is Boozuka based?+

Boozuka is based in Ludhiana, Punjab, India, and works with businesses across Punjab and India, in person and online.

What services does Boozuka offer?+

AI automation (WhatsApp chatbots, website chatbots, lead capture to CRM and follow-up reminders), website development, Google SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation, Meta and Google ads, CRM and custom software, and video editing.

Does Boozuka work with coaching institutes?+

Yes. Coaching, IELTS and skill-training institutes are a core focus: admission-focused websites, lead ads, WhatsApp follow-up and an admissions CRM.

How do I get a quote from Boozuka?+

Fill the 2-minute growth plan form at boozuka.com/get-quote.html or message +91 82890 94569 on WhatsApp. Starting prices are listed on the pricing page.

Do you outsource?+

No. Development, SEO and marketing, video editing and support are all done by our own in-house team, led by founder Jashanpreet Singh.

How long does a project take?+

A business website usually takes 2–4 weeks; CRM and automation projects 3–6 weeks, depending on scope. You get a written timeline before we start.

Who owns the website and data?+

You do. Domain, code, content and all lead data belong to your business.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. Sales depend on many things we don’t control. What we commit to is clear work, clear reporting and quick fixes when something isn’t working.

What support do I get after launch?+

Monthly plans include ongoing support. One-off projects include a support window, then optional care plans.

How does payment work?+

Usually 50% to start and 50% at launch for projects; monthly plans are billed in advance.

Do you work outside Punjab?+

Yes. Across India, and remotely with clients in Canada, the UK and Australia.
